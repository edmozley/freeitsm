## What this release is about

1.0.0 said sixteen modules had been rebuilt to work properly on a phone, with the rest following. This is the rest. The Network Mapper, the Workflow builder and the whole of System administration - fifty-three screens on its own - now work on a small screen, which takes the count to nineteen modules and a hundred and thirty-six pages.

The theme underneath it is that a diagram and a settings screen are not things people expected to use on a phone, and that turns out to be a habit rather than a fact. You can now open a network diagram on the way to a site visit, tap any box and read what it is bound to in the CMDB. You can build a workflow standing up. You can run Database Verification from the car park.

Nothing in this release changes your database, your configuration or your files, so upgrading asks nothing of you beyond the usual three steps.

## New features

- **Build a network diagram with your finger.** Press and hold a class in the CMDB panel and it lifts off, the panel slides out of the way so you can see the map, and you drop it where you want it. Press and hold a box already on the map to move it. A box's details panel gains a **Delete**, which is the part that makes the rest safe - until now removing a box was bound to the Delete key and nothing else, so on a phone a box of the wrong type was permanent. A short drag is still a short drag: the map pans and the list scrolls exactly as before. Drawing connectors between boxes is still a desktop job.

## Improvements

- **The Network Mapper works on a phone.** Find a diagram, open it, pan and zoom around it, and tap any box to read its CMDB record, follow the link through to the object, switch versions, present it or export it. The class panel used to take two thirds of a phone screen and leave 119 pixels for the diagram itself.

- **The Workflow builder works on a phone, and there is nothing you cannot reach.** Steps are added with toolbar buttons, positioned automatically, configured in a panel and deleted with a real button, so the whole of building a rule already worked by touch - it just did not fit. The configuration panel is a fixed column that is never closed, and on a 360-pixel screen it took everything and left the diagram twenty pixels. It now stacks: the diagram across the top, the panel filling the rest, and tapping a step brings it into view rather than leaving it behind the panel. The rules list and the execution log become one card per row, and the log's filter panel - which was taller than the screen before the log itself began - is half the height.

- **The whole of System administration works on a phone.** Fifty-three screens: the landing page, every administration card, the fourteen debug tools, and the guide with its nineteen topics. The landing page could not be scrolled at all - five thousand pixels of cards with no way to reach anything past the first screen. Wide administration tables such as API keys and single sign-on providers scroll sideways keeping their headings, the way every settings table already does, while the guides go the other way and stack, because you cannot scroll a sentence sideways. Branding had six boxes for your header and footer text squeezed to fifty-five pixels each, which is not wide enough to type a company name into.

## Fixes

- **Signing in with an authenticator app sent you to a page that does not exist.** After typing the six-digit code you landed on a blank error rather than your inbox, and had to type the address by hand. Ordinary sign-in was never affected, so switching on multi-factor authentication quietly changed where you ended up. Installs running FreeITSM in a subfolder are now handled properly rather than by accident. Reported by mbsouth in [issue #132](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/132).

- **The numbered contents strip on every in-app guide did nothing when tapped on a phone.** Every module's guide carries a row of numbered sections along the top, and on a small screen tapping one moved nothing and the highlight never left **1** - so the strip looked like navigation and was, in practice, decoration. Fixed once for all seventeen guides.

- **The War Room conversation could not be scrolled on a phone.** Everything was there and in the right place, and the messages simply would not move, so you could only ever read the first screenful. It now scrolls to the end.

- **The webhook delivery log ran off the side of a phone.** The log at the bottom of **System → Webhooks** is nine columns wide, and its headings ran well past the edge of the screen even when the log was empty. Each delivery is now a card carrying the workflow that sent it, when it went, the endpoint, the response code and the next retry, with **View** and **Replay** beneath.

- **The Network Mapper guide could only be read as far as the first screenful.** Its eight sections ran to over nine thousand pixels and nothing on the page scrolled at all, with no cut-off edge to suggest anything was missing.

## Still to come

Reporting, the self-service portal and the sign-in screens are the significant parts that have not been through this yet. The portal is the one that matters most and it is next.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image - or, if you pin a version, change it to `ghcr.io/edmozley/freeitsm:1.1.0`.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5). This release changes a lot of cached stylesheets and scripts, so this step matters more than usual - a stale stylesheet on a phone looks exactly like a broken layout.

Nothing else needs doing by hand. No new environment variables, no permissions to set, no scheduled tasks to create.

## Rollback

Safe - this release does not change the database at all, so you can go back to 1.0.0 by checking out the older tag or changing the pinned image number.
