## What this release is about

Two more modules now work properly on a phone, the same way Tickets, Assets, Contracts and the rest already do: **Reporting** (the landing page, System logs, the Intune dashboard and the guide) and the **System Wiki** (browse, search, database tables, scan management and the three detail pages). Nothing changes on a desktop.

Several of those pages were worse than merely cramped. **Nine of them could not be scrolled at all** — they showed the first screenful and nothing else, whatever you did. The Reporting landing page was showing one and a half of its three links, with the first one entirely off the left of the screen and no way to reach it. Both are fixed.

This is a patch release. There is nothing to configure and nothing new to learn.

## Improvements

- **Reporting on a phone.** The four-item navigation moves into the menu behind the ☰ button, and the two log tables and the Intune device list become a card per row, with each value printed against the column heading it needs — an IP address, a user agent, an attachment list, or a bare **Yes** under **Encrypted**.

- **The System Wiki on a phone.** The folder tree moves into a full-screen panel opened by a **Folders** button, because a 280px tree beside the file list left the list 80px of the screen. Tapping a folder that has sub-folders opens them and keeps the panel up; tapping one that does not closes it and shows you the files. The file list, the database-tables list and the scan history each become a card per row, and a file's page keeps the three of its six tables that already read well as tables.

- **The Intune dashboard is usable on a small screen.** Tapping a figure — compliant, encrypted, stale, newly enrolled — used to open a six-column table inside a small box floating in the middle of the screen, so every row needed a sideways drag and the Export and Close buttons ran off the edge. It now fills the screen, each device is a card, and **Export CSV** and **Close** sit on a row of their own. The five figures above are two to a row rather than five, which gives back most of a screen's height they were spending on labels wrapping to three lines.

## Fixes

- **The Reporting landing page was showing one and a half cards out of three.** Its three links sat side by side on a row wider than a phone, centred, so the row overhung on both sides: **System Logs was entirely off the left of the screen with no way to scroll to it**, and Intune was cut off on the right. They now stack and take the full width.

- **The Reporting guide could not be scrolled on a phone.** All six sections were there — seven thousand pixels of them — inside a screen that showed the first seven hundred and refused to move. The numbered contents strip along the top was tappable and did nothing at the same time.

- **Six of the eight System Wiki pages could not be scrolled either.** Each is built as a full-height panel sized to the screen, which on a phone means anything past the first screenful is unreachable — so a file's page showed its heading and its first few functions and stopped. All six now scroll.

- **The System Wiki's collapsing left panel was a dead end on a phone.** With the left panel set to appear **on hover**, the whole folder tree was sealed inside a 16px strip that a finger cannot open. It is now always reachable on a small screen, whichever setting you use, and the setting is unchanged on a desktop.

- **The System Wiki's scan history was seven unlabelled values in a column.** Each scan's date, status, duration and three counts were stacked one per line with nothing to say which figure was which. The date now leads, the status keeps its badge, and every figure carries its heading.

- **"Reply" in the email import log was drawn as a badge with no colour in it.** The log marks each imported email as either **New Ticket** or **Reply**, and only the first was given a tone, so **Reply** appeared as an empty pill — the same size and shape as its neighbour with nothing to make it look like a label. This was the same on the desktop and is corrected everywhere.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5) — this release changes stylesheets and scripts, and a cached copy will look unchanged.

No database changes, so **Database Verification** is not required this time.

## Rollback

Safe — nothing was removed from the database, so you can go back to 1.1.1 at any time.
