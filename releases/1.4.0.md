## What this release is about

**The LMS could only ever train analysts. It can now train anybody.** A course can be assigned to a group of analysts as before, to a group holding analysts and self-service portal users together, to one named person, or to **everyone on the self-service portal** in one instruction. Portal users get a **Training** tab of their own and take the course in the portal - the same player, the same knowledge checks, the same marking - and their results sit beside everyone else's on the Progress tab. That makes company-wide mandatory training, security awareness and the like, something you can actually run from FreeITSM.

**Training reminders come with it, and they are off until you switch them on.** Analysts see their courses whenever they use FreeITSM; somebody who only uses the portal has no reason to sign in unless you tell them. So FreeITSM can email people as a deadline approaches and again after it passes. Before you turn it on, the screen tells you exactly how many emails would go out right now.

**And there is a new place to keep groups of people.** Tickets → Users has a **Groups** tab: name a group, put analysts and portal users in it together, optionally give each person an end date. Those groups are shared - the same group can be given a knowledge folder or a training course.

This is a minor release. Nothing is removed, and nothing is required of you beyond the usual upgrade steps.

## New features

- **Groups of people, with an end date.** Under **Tickets → Users → Groups**. Put analysts and portal users in the same group, and give any membership a last day: three engineers on site for a fortnight drop out of the group by themselves when it passes, so the access is taken away by the clock rather than by somebody remembering. An expired membership stays listed, marked as expired, so you can still see who had access and until when. Anyone with the Tickets module can see the groups; only an administrator can change one, because adding somebody to a group can also grant them a knowledge folder.

- **Assign a course to almost anyone.** **Who for** on an assignment now offers a learning group, one of the new people groups, **Everyone on the portal**, or **One person** - which opens a search, because a list of every portal user is not something you can pick from. The number beside each group tells you how many people it will reach.

- **A Training tab in the self-service portal.** Portal users see what they have been asked to complete, how far through they are and when it is due, and take the course there. It is the same player and the same marking as the analyst side, so a pass earned in the portal is the same record as one earned in the app. The tab only appears for somebody who actually has training, so a portal that never uses this never grows a tab leading nowhere.

- **Outstanding training on the portal dashboard.** Cards above the ticket counts, soonest first with anything overdue in front. Only what is still to do is listed, and the whole panel is absent for anybody with nothing outstanding.

- **Training reminders.** **LMS → Settings → Reminders**: choose how many days before a deadline to remind, whether to keep chasing afterwards and how often. Nobody is reminded twice about the same deadline, and reminders stop the moment somebody finishes the course. **Off by default** - the first run after an upgrade is exactly when a helpful default would email several hundred people about training assigned months ago. Before you switch it on you are shown how many reminders would be sent right now, and how many people have no email address to reach.

## Improvements

- **Reminders work without setting up a scheduled task.** FreeITSM sends anything due when somebody opens the LMS, at most once an hour, so this works out of the box. That is a fallback rather than a schedule - if nobody opens the LMS for a week, nothing is sent for a week - so for reliable daily sending, run `cron/lms_reminders.php` once a day.

- **Assignments can be edited.** Getting the group or the deadline wrong used to mean deleting the row and starting again. Nobody's progress is affected by an edit: training records are held against the person and the course, never against the assignment.

- **Training reminders are their own line in the outbound email log**, rather than being filed under Portal alongside password resets, so "did the chase go out, and to whom" is answerable.

- **The guides are up to date.** The LMS guide gains sections on training in the portal and on reminders; the portal's own user guide gains a Training section; the Tickets guide documents the Groups tab.

## Fixes

- **A course was marked overdue on the morning of the day it was due.** Anybody told they had until Friday opened it on Friday to find they were already late. A deadline is now a day: you have all of it.

- **Two screens disagreed about the same due date.** The same course could be due on the 1st on one screen and the 2nd on another, depending on your timezone.

- **The portal's Training page could show an administrator their own analyst courses.** Anyone signed into both the analyst app and the portal - in two tabs, say - was shown the wrong list, and taking a course from the portal would have recorded it against the wrong account.

- **A knowledge folder could be shared with a "group" that could not exist.** The folder permission picker has always offered groups, and everything behind that option worked, but there was no way anywhere in FreeITSM to create one. The new Groups tab is the missing screen.

- **An access end date could be shown as the wrong day**, and the date box emptied itself between adding one person and the next - so adding three contractors for the same fortnight gave the second and third permanent access.

- **The LMS console could not be scrolled at all.** On the Progress tab that meant 49 rows existed and only the first screenful could ever be reached.

- **The end of a course looked like two unrelated screens.** Finishing showed a "Course complete" banner and then a separate grey strip holding a lone button. It is now a single result with a **Finish** underneath it.

- **Smaller things on the portal:** the Training page flashed a "Loading" box on every refresh; a course nobody had opened showed an empty progress bar reading "Lesson 0 of 3"; Popular articles sat flush against the panel above it on the dashboard; and a portal page could not add to its own set of translations, which left some labels on the course player showing as internal names.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. This release adds tables and columns and moves two database keys, so it is not optional.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5). This release updates cached JavaScript and stylesheets.

Nothing else is required. If you want reminder emails, switch them on under **LMS → Settings → Reminders** - and add a daily scheduled task for `cron/lms_reminders.php` if you want them sent reliably rather than opportunistically.

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was removed from the database, so you can go back to 1.3.0.

One caveat if you do: assignments made to the new targets (a people group, everyone on the portal, or one named person) are stored in the same column the old version reads as a learning group, so 1.3.0 would misread them. Delete any such assignment before rolling back, or expect the wrong people to be listed against those courses until you come forward again.
