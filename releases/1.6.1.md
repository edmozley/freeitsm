## What this release is about

Three fixes, all found in one evening's testing. The first is the one to upgrade for.

When an analyst attached a file to a reply, the file was sent correctly and the requester received it - but FreeITSM kept no record of it. Nothing appeared on the ticket, and the file was not stored at all. The reply itself, the recipient and the text were all saved as normal. Only the attachment was lost.

The other two are cosmetic: folders in the ticket list were showing two icons where they should show one, and a diagnostic screen printed a time that looked an hour out.

## Fixes

- **Attachments on replies were sent but not kept.** A file attached to a reply went out with the email and left no trace on the ticket: no attachment shown, nothing stored. So the person you were helping had the document in their inbox, while the next colleague to open that ticket saw a reply referring to something that, as far as FreeITSM was concerned, never existed. That is a problem for anyone treating the ticket as the record of what was sent - which is most of the point of having one.

  Attachments on replies are now stored exactly like attachments on incoming mail and portal replies, and appear against the message they went out with.

  **Affected versions: every release up to and including 1.6.0.** Attachments that arrived *into* FreeITSM were always kept correctly, and attachments already on your tickets are untouched. This only ever affected files going out.

  **If you need to recover one:** the file was never saved, so FreeITSM cannot produce it - but the copy in your own Sent folder is intact, since the email itself was correct. Any reply sent before upgrading will still show no attachment on the ticket; only replies sent from now on are recorded.

- **Folders in the ticket list showed two icons.** **All Tickets**, **Unassigned**, **Snoozed**, **Trash** and the analyst and team folders each drew a plain folder *and* their own icon - a folder and a bin, a folder and a moon. They now show only their own. Department folders are unchanged and still open and close as you expand them.

- **The email import log showed a time that looked an hour wrong.** On the **Logs** screen, opening an entry's details showed a timestamp that disagreed with the Date/time column beside it. Neither was actually wrong: the column is shown in your own timezone, while the details are printed exactly as stored, which is always UTC. Times in that panel now say so, and show your local equivalent alongside - so the two can be read together instead of appearing to contradict each other. This only ever affected what was displayed on that one screen.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5). This release changes the ticket screen's stylesheet.

There is no database change in this release.

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was added to or removed from the database, so you can go back to 1.6.0 at any time.
