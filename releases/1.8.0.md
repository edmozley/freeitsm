## What this release is about

Two unrelated things, both about not typing the same information twice.

The bigger one is **people**. A person in FreeITSM has always had somewhere to record a job title, an office, a phone number and who they report to — and on the screen most analysts actually use, there was no way to see any of it, let alone type it in. Those details now appear wherever you look somebody up, and more usefully, **a customer can correct their own** from the self-service portal. A phone number that has changed is a phone number you cannot reach, and routing every correction through a ticket means most of them never get made.

The other is the **staff rota**. Building next week by hand, cell by cell, when it is the same as this week was the kind of job that makes people keep the rota in a spreadsheet instead. You can now copy a shift and paste it elsewhere, or copy an entire week and drop it onto another one.

There is also a fix worth knowing about if you have ever had a dialogue box that seemed to have no bottom to it: some of them genuinely did not, and could not be scrolled.

## New features

- **A person's contact details, everywhere you look them up.** Job title, department, office, phone, mobile, employee ID and who they report to now appear on **Tickets → Users** as well as **Assets → Users**, so it no longer matters which module you came in through — the same person shows the same details either way. All optional. Two are worth filling in for reasons beyond neatness: **office** tells you which site somebody is at before you set off looking for them, and **employee ID** is what a payroll reconciliation needs the day somebody asks for one. Blank fields are left out of a person's summary rather than shown as a column of dashes.

- **Your customers can keep their own details current.** A signed-in portal user opens **My Account** and can now maintain their **job title, office, phone and mobile** themselves, beside their preferred name. They are told, plainly, that only the IT team can see them. Department, employee ID and manager are deliberately not offered — nobody should be able to nominate their own approver, and a payroll number is a claim about who somebody is rather than a way of reaching them. Requested in [issue #133](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/133).

- **Where you use a staff directory, those details come from it.** If somebody was imported from Active Directory or LDAP, their details are shown but read-only — on both analyst screens and in the portal — and each says why. That is a deliberate refusal rather than a gap: the next sync would overwrite anything typed locally, and an edit that quietly reverts overnight is how people conclude the system lost their change. Change it in the directory and it arrives on the next run.

- **Copy and paste on the staff rota.** Right-click a cell to **Copy** the shift in it and right-click another to **Paste**. If the target already has something, you are asked before it is replaced.

- **Copy a whole week of the rota onto another week.** **Copy week** puts the week on the clipboard and a **Paste week** button appears, naming which week it is holding — by the time you have navigated three weeks away, a bare "Paste week" is a guess. Replacing a week that already has entries asks first, with numbers, and replace genuinely means replace rather than merge: *"This week has 5 entries already. Pasting replaces the whole week with the 5 you copied, so the 5 currently here are removed."* Nothing is lost quietly either — if a shift or a location has been retired since you copied, you are told how many entries could not be placed and why.

## Improvements

- **Directory-managed records say so before you try to edit them.** The note explaining that somebody's details come from your staff directory is now tinted and marked, rather than grey text easily read past — it is the only thing that explains why the boxes below will not accept typing.

- **Pasting a week is one operation.** Seven analysts across seven days used to be up to 49 separate saves, where the first failure left half a week behind with no way to tell which half. It either looks like the week you copied, or it is untouched.

## Fixes

- **Some dialogue boxes could not be scrolled, and their bottoms were unreachable.** Where a form grew taller than the window, **Save** and **Cancel** stayed pinned at the bottom as normal — so the box looked complete — while the last few fields sat below the visible area with no scrollbar to reach them. **New asset** in the asset module was affected, and so was the person editor this release adds fields to. The cause had been there since the current dialogue layout landed and only showed up once a form was long enough to overflow, so a third dialogue built the same way was one field away from it.

- **Pasting a rota week could have deleted entries nobody could see.** Replacing a week means clearing what the copied week did not have, and the obvious way to do that would also have removed shifts belonging to deactivated analysts, and shifts on days you have chosen not to display. The clearing is now scoped on the server to exactly what you were shown.

- **Saving your phone number in the portal wiped your preferred name.** Anything saved without also re-sending the preferred name cleared it — the name FreeITSM uses to address you in every email it sends. Deliberately clearing it still works; forgetting to mention it no longer does.

- **A person's manager could have been cleared without anyone seeing it happen.** On installations with more than one company, if somebody's manager was in a company you cannot see, the manager box appeared empty — and saving the page would have recorded "no manager", removing a reporting line you were never shown. The same applies to the manager's name, which is no longer read across company boundaries at all.

- **The help said approvals follow the manager chain. They do not.** Catalogue request approvals go to an analyst named on the form. The reporting line is recorded so it is ready if that changes, and the help now says that instead of promising something that does not happen.

- **The portal's account dialogue was a sheet of white in dark mode.** **My Account** and **Multi-Factor Auth** had been written in fixed light colours, so anyone using the portal's dark theme got a glaring white panel with dark input boxes inside it. Both now follow the theme properly, along with the account dropdown behind them. The QR code for setting up MFA deliberately keeps its white background — a phone camera needs it to scan.

- **A portal user signed in through a staff directory was shown locked fields with no explanation.** The note telling them their details come from their organisation's directory was present but not being displayed at all, so the boxes simply refused to accept typing for no visible reason.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. **Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).** This one matters more than usual: the dialogue-scrolling fix is in a stylesheet, so a browser holding the old one keeps the bug.
3. Running **System → Database Verification** does no harm and is a good habit, but this release adds no tables and no columns — the contact detail columns have existed since the directory sync work, which is the reason this was a screen to build rather than a schema change.

Nothing else to do. No new settings, no scheduled tasks, no configuration changes.

## Rollback

Safe. Nothing was removed from the database and no columns changed shape, so you can go back to 1.7.0 without losing anything. Contact details already recorded simply stop being visible until you upgrade again.
