## What this release is about

A small release about using FreeITSM on a phone. The ticket screens lose several rough edges: buttons that ran off the side of the screen, a link that opened a ticket behind the list instead of in front of it, and a "Link to task" that seemed to do nothing. Contracts gets the same sticky footer the other phone-friendly modules have, and the competency Tests pages in the LMS now work properly on a phone. Nothing changes on a desktop.

## Improvements

- **Contracts on a phone has a sticky footer.** Overview (the figures), Contracts (the list), Search, Add and Filter sit along the bottom of the screen, as they do in other modules, instead of everything being stacked on one long page.
- **Competency tests work on a phone.** The tests list, the question bank, candidates, the test builder and a candidate's result now fit a phone screen. Lists become cards, the builder rearranges itself, and fields are big enough that the phone does not zoom in.
- **Attachments on a phone are shown as cards.** Each attachment gets a full-width card with its preview, filename, who sent it, when, and its size, instead of a table squeezed onto a small screen.
- **Users on a phone.** A user's People, Edit, Manager access and Delete actions are now icons in a sticky footer, and their tickets are listed as small cards.
- **Linking tickets on a phone.** The Links entry in a ticket's menu shows how many links the ticket has, for example "Links (2)", and the lists for linking a problem, change or ticket fill the screen instead of stopping halfway down.
- **The checklist template editor on a phone** uses less padding, and each step puts its role on its own line under the title instead of squeezing both onto one row.

## Fixes

- **On a phone, opening a ticket from a link left you on the ticket list.** Tapping one of a user's tickets, a notification, "Open in inbox" in the Calendar or a linked-ticket pill loaded the ticket behind the list. It now opens the ticket.
- **On a phone, "Link to task" seemed to do nothing.** It now opens a full-screen list of tasks, the same as linking a change, and lists tasks straight away, open ones first, before you type a search.
- **On a phone, the Attach, Suggest, Summarise and Send buttons under a chat reply** (Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack or web chat) ran off the right of the screen. They now sit in a two-by-two grid.
- **On a phone, Users could not be scrolled.** The selected user's details are scrollable again, the action buttons no longer run off the screen, and very long names are shortened with an ellipsis.
- **On a phone, the CSAT page's Response rate card ran off the screen.** The headline figures now sit two across, with Response rate on its own row.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5) - this release updates cached JavaScript and styles, and an old copy on your phone will look like nothing changed. On a phone, close and reopen the browser tab.

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was changed in the database, so you can go back to 3.1.1 with the code alone.
