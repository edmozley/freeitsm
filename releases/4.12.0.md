## What this release is about

People imported from Microsoft Entra ID now bring their groups with them, so you can see who is in Finance without opening the Entra admin centre, and a person a directory keeps up to date can no longer be deleted by accident. A company can now say which other companies it supports, for service providers whose staff raise tickets on behalf of a client. Knowledge gets an article picker for links, links that open where you expect, and a much quicker Back to list. And a batch of fixes and small improvements from people's own lists, including notifications when a task is dragged on the board.

## New features

- **Groups from Microsoft Entra ID in People.** Groups imported from Entra ID get a **Group** filter on the People list, a **Groups** tab, a page for each group, and show on a person's page and a company's page. Sign-in group mappings show each group's name instead of an object id. Contributed by vsc55.
- **One company supporting another.** **System → Companies** lets a company say which companies it supports. **Don't warn** stops the "wrong company" warning on tickets its staff raise for those clients, and **Portal** lets its staff choose which company a ticket is for when they raise it in the self-service portal. A person still belongs to one company. Raised by duongtuanan.
- **Link one Knowledge article to another without knowing its number.** In the editor, **Link** has a browse button beside the address that searches your articles, shows each one's folder, and fills the link in. Such links open in the self-service Help Centre too, for requesters allowed to read the article. Contributed by Dr4inder.
- **Everybody under a manager, counted.** The organisation chart shows each manager's team and, after a dot, how many people are under them in all - "2 · 13". The side panel and the CSV, JSON and XML exports carry both numbers. Contributed by vsc55.
- **Reply goes to.** **Tickets → Settings → General → Reply goes to** chooses who Reply is addressed to: the person on the message you are reading, as it has always been, or always the ticket's requester - for when colleagues join a requester's email thread. Raised by Kraleemil.
- **Leave collected mail in the mailbox.** A mailbox's **Imported emails** setting can now be **Mark as read and leave it in the folder**, for a mailbox people also read in Outlook or Thunderbird. Raised by pappas81.
- **Involve me.** Right-click a task on the board and choose **Involve me** to follow it without owning it; **Stop being involved** takes you off again. Raised by pappas81.
- **Set a ticket field from a workflow.** A new **Set a ticket field** action fills one of your custom ticket fields, and the value can be built from other fields or an earlier step, so a field can be worked out automatically. Raised by pappas81.

## Improvements

- **Back to list in Knowledge is instant**, and keeps the folder, search and tag filters you were using, instead of reloading the whole page.
- **The Knowledge editor's pop-out** gives the editor the space: properties, documents, domains and projects sit in one tidy column beside it, without boxes inside boxes.
- **Workflows can use any field of a record.** `{{task.full.ticket_id}}` - any field of a record as the REST API returns it - now works in every workflow action, not only webhooks. Never for a confidential ticket.
- **The organisation chart's mouse wheel scrolls** the page, and Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) with the wheel, or a trackpad pinch, zooms. Switching between Cards and Compact keeps your zoom, and a new **Mouse and keyboard** button lists every gesture. Contributed by vsc55.
- **Each directory source says when it is imported**, the Entra run history says who joined and left each group, and a person a directory keeps up to date cannot be deleted by hand. Contributed by vsc55.
- **German:** the ticket owner is now "Bearbeiter", which no longer reads as the person who raised the ticket. Contributed by Dr4inder.

## Fixes

- **Dragging a task to another status on the board told nobody** - no notification, no email, no workflow - while changing it from the task's menu told everybody. Moving tasks through the REST API had the same gap. Reported by pappas81.
- **A link to a Knowledge article sometimes did not open** - on any install whose top level held only folders, or in any language other than English. It now opens, in the article's own folder. Fixed by Dr4inder.
- **Knowledge article previews showed codes like `&auml;`** instead of letters, in the article list, the hover preview and the REST API. Fixed by Dr4inder.
- **Full screen from the Knowledge editor's pop-out** collapsed the editor to its toolbar.
- **Collecting mail and opening a ticket got slower as ticket history grew.** Two lookups read every email FreeITSM had ever stored; they are now indexed.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. It adds the table for company support, a column on tickets, and two indexes on emails - on a large install, adding the indexes takes a moment.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5) - this release updates the Knowledge, Tickets, Tasks and People pages.

If you schedule the directory import yourself, its script is now `cron/directory_sync.php`, with the other scheduled tasks. The old path, `scripts/directory_sync.php`, still works, so nothing needs changing today.

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was removed from the database, so you can go back to 4.11.0.
