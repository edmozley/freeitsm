## What this release is about

Five new things, most of them asked for by people running FreeITSM. Domains can now watch **internal names** - an intranet, an internal application - without ever sending them outside your network. Assets that have left the estate can be **retired**: kept as a record, with the date and the reason, but out of your lists and counts. A **screenshot pasted into a ticket note** stays in the note where you put it. The **ticket table** uses the whole width of the screen and opens a ticket beside it. And Report Packs gain **Change Management** blocks and a list of retired assets.

## New features

- **Internal domains.** Set a domain's **Scope** to **Internal** and it is never sent outside your network: no public DNS, no registry lookup, no Certificate Transparency watch and no look-alike scan, not even as a fallback when something fails. Registration and email checks no longer apply, so an intranet is no longer graded F. Instead it gets a **health** from your own server's DNS - Healthy, Problem, Not checked or Stale - with the date of the last completed check and when it was last healthy. Internal domains are checked every hour by default (**Domains → Settings → Monitoring**). Designed with and requested by @Dr4inder ([#203](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/203)).
- **Retired assets.** Tick **Retired** on a status under **Assets → Settings → Asset statuses**, and any asset given that status is stamped with the date and can carry a reason, such as "Transferred to the Ministry of Education". It then leaves the asset list, the Table, dashboards, Watchtower, warranty and lease alerts and the calendar, but keeps its record. **Show retired** brings them back, and the Table's **Retired on** and **Reason** columns export to CSV or PDF - the list to send with a transfer. A retired machine that still reports in is recognised, so no duplicate is made, but its record is not changed. Asked for by a supporter of the project in public-sector IT.
- **Paste a screenshot into a ticket note.** Press Ctrl+V in the Add note box and the picture goes in where your cursor is, and stays there once saved - in the ticket and, on a shared note, in the portal. Click it to open it in the viewer. Requested by @Dr4inder ([#224](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/224)).
- **The ticket table uses the whole width.** With no ticket open there is no reading pane, rather like Outlook with its reading pane off. Click a ticket, or press Enter, and it opens beside the table with a divider you can drag; your browser remembers where you left it. **Back** or Escape gives the table its width back without losing anything you had half-written. Contributed by @Dr4inder ([PR #223](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/pull/223)).
- **Change Management and retired assets in Report Packs.** New blocks show changes by status, type or risk (also priority and impact), and a list of the period's changes, raised or scheduled in it. The **Retired assets** block lists what left the estate in the period, with type, serial number, date and reason.

## Improvements

- **Full screen is a proper focused view.** The bar above a full-screen ticket shows its reference and a **Back** button instead of the inbox controls. Escape steps back one level at a time. Enter steps in one level: on the table, the ticket opens beside it, then full screen. The arrow keys scroll whatever you are looking at - the ticket, or a picture or PDF window - instead of moving between tickets you cannot see. Leaving full screen puts the keyboard back on the list. Requested by @Dr4inder ([#225](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/225)).
- **On the table shown on its own, the arrow keys only move the highlight**, so the screen does not jump on every key press. A click or Enter opens the ticket, and once one is open the arrows show each ticket as you move.
- **Scrolling down a ticket folds the header away smoothly** instead of jumping.
- **The Add note dialog** is a fixed size that fits without scrolling, the cursor is in the note box as soon as it opens, and **Share with the requester** is a switch.
- **German and Spanish are complete again**, including the Case report, which had never been translated.

## Fixes

- **A mailbox that had ever received mail could not be deleted** - Delete failed with "1451 ... fk_emails_mailbox", even after every ticket was purged. Delete now opens a dialog that shows what is attached and moves the emails to another mailbox, or clears them, in one step ([#227](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/227)). Thanks to @bigtrodat for the report.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. This release adds columns for internal domains and retired assets; until it is run, neither can be used.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5). This release updates cached scripts on the Tickets, Assets, Domains and Report Packs pages.

Things to know:

- **Nothing changes until you choose it.** Every domain stays Public, and no status counts as Retired, until you set it.
- **Internal domains are checked through DNS only for now.** Certificates on internal hosts are not checked yet; that arrives in a later release, together with the protections it needs. Checking through "this server's DNS" keeps FreeITSM's questions on your server - if that DNS server forwards questions it cannot answer to a public service, it is the DNS server that sends them out.
- **REST API:** once a status is ticked Retired, `GET /assets` leaves retired assets out; `?retired=include` returns every asset and `?retired=only` just the retired ones. Domains carry `scope` and `health`, and the list accepts `?scope=`.

## Rollback

Safe for your data: Database Verification only adds columns, and 4.4.0 ignores them. One caution - 4.4.0 does not know about internal domains, so it would treat any you have added as public and look them up outside your network again. If you go back, first remove those domains, or edit each one and switch its **Monitoring** off, so the scheduled checks leave it alone.
