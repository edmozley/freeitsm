## What this release is about

One new module and one big phone improvement. **Starters & leavers** gives every person joining, changing role or leaving a case of their own. Its steps land on the Tasks board, with checklists where you want them. Kit is issued and recovered through Assets, and a leaver can be switched off in one click. The questions you already ask, such as "Do they need a laptop?" or "Which document system?", can come from a form of your own, and the answers decide which steps are needed. **Tasks** on a phone now has every filter in reach.

## New features

- **Starters & leavers.** Open a case for a starter, a mover or a leaver, with their date and the template to follow.
  - **Templates** live under **Starters & leavers → Settings**. Each step can have a team, an analyst, a due date counted from the person's date, and a checklist.
  - Each step becomes a task on the **Tasks** board, so your team works it where they already work. The case shows its progress as steps are closed.
  - **Kit** is issued to a starter and recovered from a leaver through Assets, with the usual custody history. Recovered kit can be retired in the same step.
  - **Left** switches the person's account off.
  - The case has documents, a history, and two workflow triggers: case opened and case completed.
  - Each person's page in **People** lists their cases.
- **Questions from your own forms.** A template can use a form from the Forms module. The answers can:
  - **pick the template** by a rule, such as "Department is Dispute Resolution" choosing a "Legal starter" template;
  - **leave out steps** the answers rule out, such as no mailbox step when "Private mailbox?" is No;
  - **fill in step wording**, such as "Set up {Document system} access".

  A manager can fill the form in on the portal: add **Open a Starters & leavers case** to the form's actions and the case opens by itself. Or the analyst can open the case and answer the form on its task. Either way, the answers show on the case, each with a **Copy** button.

  Forms are optional. A template can use a form, a checklist, both or neither.

## Improvements

- **Tasks on a phone.** Search, the view switches, team, analyst, project, contractor and tag filters were squeezed into one line or missing altogether. They now open in a full-screen panel from a **Filters** bar at the bottom of the screen. The bar shows what you are looking at and how many filters are on.
- **German and Spanish** cover the new module from day one. The tasks a case creates are written in the case owner's language, so a German team sees German task titles on its board.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. This release adds four tables for Starters & leavers. The module cannot be used until Database Verification has run.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5). This release updates cached scripts on the Tasks pages.

Things to know:

- **Give people access.** Starters & leavers is a new module, so grant it to the analysts who need it. Editing templates and cancelling other analysts' cases have their own permissions.
- **To answer a form on the case's task**, turn on **Forms on tasks** under **Tasks → Settings**. It is off by default.

## Rollback

Safe: nothing was removed from the database, so you can go back to 4.5.0. The four new tables are simply ignored.
