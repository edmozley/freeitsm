## What this release is about

If your organisation signs in with Microsoft, FreeITSM can now read your people and groups from Microsoft Entra ID. You can give a new starter a laptop or raise a ticket for them before they have ever signed in, and most Microsoft 365 organisations have no LDAP directory to use instead. It only reads from Entra, and it does nothing until you switch it on.

## New features

- **Import people from Microsoft Entra ID.** Bring in name, email, job title, department, phones and manager, for everybody or for the members of the groups you choose. You can choose which Entra field fills each one. Somebody disabled in Entra is marked as having left.
- **Import groups.** See who is in each Entra group, including members of groups nested inside it. The group names also replace the long IDs in your sign-in group mappings.
- **Imported people sign in straight away.** Their first Microsoft sign-in lands on the record the import made for them.
- **A reused sign-in name or email is flagged, not merged.** When a leaver's address goes to somebody new, the import shows a conflict and leaves the old record alone, so nobody inherits another person's tickets. The new starter is not imported until the two are separated, and there is no screen for that yet.

Find it under **System → Authentication**, or through the new **Microsoft Entra ID** card under **System → Integrations**. Press **Preview** first: it shows what would happen to each person and changes nothing. There is no undo for an import, so read it before your first one. Nobody is ever deleted, and an import never creates an analyst. Setup is in [Importing people from Microsoft Entra ID](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/wiki/Importing-People-from-Entra-ID).

## Fixes

- **Directory imports no longer report "Reporting lines 1 set" on every run** when nothing has changed.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).

Reading Entra needs an app registration with the Microsoft Graph application permissions `User.Read.All` and `GroupMember.Read.All`, and a client secret. Nothing changes until you switch an import on.

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was removed from the database, so you can go back to 4.6.1. People and groups already imported stay in place.
