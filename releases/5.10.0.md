## What this release is about

Catalogue requests can now be approved the way purchases really are: in order, by more than one person, and by the people who hold the budget rather than only by service desk staff. A request can go to the requester's manager, then the owner of the cost centre, then the board only when the order is over an amount you choose. Managers and budget holders decide on the self-service portal, with no service desk account. And a form can now work out its own order total, from prices on its options, a basket, or a bundle of catalogue items at their own prices. **Nothing changes for a form you already have** - each one keeps working exactly as before until you give it steps.

## New features

- **Approval in steps.** A form's **Approval settings** is now a list of steps, run one at a time: the IT manager, then finance, then the board. Each step is decided by a person or a team, can apply **only when** an answer matches (*Order total is more than 5000*), and has **If nobody can decide** - who it goes to when its approver has left or made the request. A step that does not apply is skipped and the record says why. The approvers of each step are told on their bell when the request reaches them, and the inbox shows "Step 2 of 3: Finance" with a trail of the steps.
- **Approval chains.** Set the same steps up once under **Forms -> Settings -> Approvals** - say *Hardware purchase* - and choose the chain on as many forms as you like. The same page sets the install's default for **If nobody can decide** (for example your Controlling team).
- **The requester's manager and cost centre owners approve on the portal.** A step can now be decided by **the requester's manager** (from the Manager field) or **the owner of a cost centre** - the requester's own, or the one chosen on the form. They approve on the portal's new **Approvals** page, signed in as themselves, are told by the portal bell, and by email when you choose a mailbox (the email links to the request; it has no approve button, so forwarding it passes nothing on). Who the manager or owner is gets checked when they decide, so a new manager can pick up a request that was waiting for the old one. Rejecting needs a reason, which the requester sees.
- **Cost centre owners and deputies.** **System -> Cost Centres** gives each cost centre an **owner** - a person or a people group, any of whose members can decide - and a **deputy**, also in the import and export (`Owner email`, `Owner group`, `Deputy email`), the REST API sync and on a person's page (*Responsible for*). **Forms -> Settings -> Approvals** decides when the deputy may decide: any time, or only as cover - when the owner has set **Away until** on the portal, has left, made the request, or after a number of days you choose.
- **Remind.** A requester can press **Remind** beside a waiting request on the portal to tell its approvers again - at most once a day per step. It can be switched off.
- **Calculated totals.** A new question type, **Calculated total**, works out an order's cost as the person answers: a fixed amount times a quantity, the value of the option chosen (dropdown and choose-one options can now carry a value - *Standard 900, Pro 1500*), a basket table's price times quantity down the rows, or a **bundle** of catalogue items at their own prices, so a price lives in one place. FreeITSM works the total out again when the form is sent and keeps that figure, so an approval step's "more than 5000" can never be got round by typing a smaller one.
- **Cost centres in form questions.** A **Lookup** question can now search your company's cost centres, and start on the requester's own.
- **Thumbnails in Files.** Pictures and the first page of PDFs show as small pictures in **Icons**, and in a new **Thumbnails** view. Each is made once and kept, so a folder of a hundred photos opens as quickly as any other. **Make thumbnail** redraws one, and **Files -> Settings -> Thumbnails** works through any backlog ([#424](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/424), thanks @proseriox).
- **Snap assist in Files.** Drag a window to the left or right edge to fill that half of the screen, and the other half offers your other open windows to fill it.

## Improvements

- **The requester's cost centre travels with the request.** The cost centre someone was charged to when they asked is kept with the request and shown to the approver and in the ticket an approval raises ([#348](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/348), thanks @vsc55).
- **Forms -> Settings no longer sends the stored AI key to the browser**, even in its encrypted form. The page never used it.

## Fixes

- **On PHP 8.5, replying to a ticket said "You do not have access to this module".** FreeITSM also no longer trips PHP 8.5's new deprecation warnings.
- **Help assistant: setting up matching by meaning with OpenAI could fail** with "maximum input length is 8192 tokens" on an install whose help is in more than one language.

Thank you to @bigtrodat, who asked for financial approvals in [#160](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/160) and answered every design question in detail - the deputy as cover, the fallback approver, reusable chains and Remind all came from those answers.

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System -> Database Verification**. It adds the approval steps, chains and cost centre owner columns.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).

Nothing else is required. To email approvers, choose a mailbox under **Forms -> Settings -> Approvals**; until you do, they are told by the bell only.

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was removed from the database, so you can go back to 5.9.1.
