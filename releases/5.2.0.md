## What this release is about

5.2.0 is a big one, and most of it came straight from people using FreeITSM. **Files** has grown up: it keeps **versions** of a file, can keep everything in **S3-compatible storage**, and its files can now be **attached to tickets, tasks, assets and projects - and opened right there**, in a window over the page, without leaving for the Files module.

On the service desk side, a person can have **more than one email address**, so a ticket sent from a private address when someone cannot get into work lands under them and shows in their portal. A ticket's **requester can be changed**. **To and Cc suggest addresses** as you type - a supplier's service desk first. And **Starters & leavers** can raise its steps as **tickets** in the teams' queues instead of tasks. Nothing is removed and nothing needs setting up beyond Database Verification.

## New features

- **Files: versions.** Upload a file with the same name and choose what happens - a new version (the default), keep both as "Report (2).pdf", overwrite, or skip - set under **Files → Settings → Versions**, along with how many versions to keep. Right-click a file for **Version history**: see every version, restore one, or delete one.
- **Files: S3-compatible storage.** **Files → Settings → Storage** can keep files in Amazon S3, Backblaze, Wasabi, MinIO and other S3-compatible services instead of a server folder, and move what is already stored from one to the other, with checks before and after. Contributed by cloudcogsio.
- **Files on tickets, tasks, assets, projects and everything with Documents.** **From Files** opens a window like a file dialog - folders on the left, their files on the right, a search - and puts the file on the record as a **link** (opened through Files, always the latest version, under its folder's permissions) or a **copy**, as **Files → Settings → Sharing** allows. On a ticket it is in the **Add note** window, beside **From Computer**.
- **Save to Files.** An email attachment on a ticket, or any document on a record, can be saved into a folder in Files - including a folder for that ticket.
- **Open documents where you are.** A document's name or **Open** shows it in a window over the page - PDFs, Word, spreadsheets, pictures, text, audio and video - instead of downloading it. Email attachments on a ticket open the same way.
- **People with more than one email address.** A person can add another address in the portal (**My account → Your email addresses**) - a link is emailed to it, and it is added when they open that link - or an analyst can link one on the person's page in **People**. Mail from it is theirs: their ticket shows in their portal. A linked address never signs anyone in. If a separate person was already made for that address, you can **merge** them in, tickets and all. A person's page now has a **History** of these changes.
- **Change a ticket's requester.** **Change requester**, beside the sender at the top of a ticket, makes it someone else's, in the ticket's history - and offers to merge the old requester in when they are clearly the same person writing from another address.
- **Addresses as you type in To and Cc.** Emailing from a ticket suggests addresses: a supplier's **service desk address** first (a new field on suppliers in Contracts), then supplier contacts, people of the ticket's company, and addresses recently emailed from that company's tickets. An address from one company is never suggested on another company's ticket.
- **Starters & leavers: steps as tickets.** Each template step can be raised as a **ticket** in the team's queue instead of a task. Steps with the same ticket name become one ticket with a checklist of those steps, scheduled on its due date, linked to the case and counted in its progress. Whether the person who asked for the case gets emails about these tickets is up to the template.
- **Working values out in step text.** In Starters & leavers steps, `{First name|initial|upper}{Last name|upper}` gives "JSMITH" - initial, first, last, upper, lower, nospace, slug and ascii.
- **The subject in Reply and Forward.** The subject is shown and can be changed, and **Reply** or **Forward** on one message in the conversation starts from that message's subject - so a follow-up keeps an outside provider's own ticket number.
- **Report Packs: Starters & leavers and assets with tickets.** A Starters & leavers area (a summary with how many were ready on the day, a chart, a list, overdue steps), and blocks for which assets - and which models - have the most tickets.
- **The REST API: what is on a ticket.** A ticket now says how many notes, messages, attachments, forms and checklists it has, and what it is linked to - tasks, problems, changes, projects, assets, other tickets and Jira issues - so an integration knows which details are worth fetching. A new endpoint lists a ticket's attachments, and a ticket's requester can be changed.
- **Photo Album: CGA, EGA and VGA.** Three more screens for the webcam art - the old PC colour palettes.

## Improvements

- **Module headers are the same height everywhere.**
- **The REST API's description matches what it returns**, for every endpoint, so generated clients type correctly.
- **Danish, German, Greek, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Polish and Ukrainian** have everything in this release.

## Fixes

- **Download PDF on a form's submissions said "The PDF could not be created"** - since 4.11.0, on every install. Fixed.
- **A webhook in "Full record" format never left for a form submission** - in a workflow on a form being received, or in a form's own "What happens next". It now sends the whole submission.
- **The password expired, forgot password and reset password pages showed no message** after the first click.
- **Saving an analyst with no email showed a database error** instead of saying the email is required.
- **Some System pages scrolled a little past the bottom of the window.**

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. It adds the tables and columns for Files versions, linked email addresses, steps as tickets and suppliers' service desk addresses.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).

Everything new is off or empty until you use it: Files keeps storing in its server folder until you choose S3, and Starters & leavers templates keep raising tasks until you change a step.

## Rollback

Safe - nothing was removed from the database, so you can go back to 5.1.0. Files stored in S3 would need moving back to the server folder first.
