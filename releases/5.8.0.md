## What this release is about

The headline is **Consumables**, a new screen in Assets for the things you hand out rather than track one by one - toner, mice, cables, batteries. Book deliveries in against an order, use stock on a ticket from its right-click menu, count the shelves, and see what went missing and what it cost. Workflows can now **branch**, the self-service portal gets an **organisation chart**, every settings page can be shown **on one page with a search**, and morning checks can run **on a schedule**. The notification bell now names what really made a change, and every row can explain why it is there.

## New features

- **Consumables (Assets → Consumables).** A stock list per company with part numbers, units, suppliers, costs and custom fields per type. Each item has a **Reorder at** level, and anything running low shows on the list, on Watchtower and, for the people you choose, in the bell. **Reorder** can open a requisition form already filled in. Everything is recorded: stock is never edited, only moved, and every movement, undo and change says who, when and why (asked for by a supporter of the project).
  - **Deliveries:** book one order with several items in at once, with an optional order number, invoice details and the paperwork attached. A whole delivery can be undone.
  - **Using stock:** right-click a ticket and choose **Use a consumable** - the first 10 items with what is in stock, and a search. What was used shows on the ticket and can be undone. A request form can also ask *which* consumable someone needs, on the portal too.
  - **Stock takes:** count the shelves and FreeITSM records each difference - expected 10, counted 8 - and its value. The **Differences** report shows losses and gains over time, in units and money.
  - Who may adjust stock or finish a stock take is set in **System → Roles**. Everyone with Assets can receive and issue.
  - **System → Demo data** has a Consumables set with four months of history to try it on.
- **Workflows can branch.** **If / Otherwise** runs the first path whose conditions match. **Choose by value** picks a path by a field's value. Both always have an Otherwise / Default path ([#364](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/364), thanks @duongtuanan).
- **A ticket's custom fields in workflow text**, by name: *{{ticket.custom.Start date}}* ([#304](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/304), thanks @Shadowtech87).
- **An organisation chart on the self-service portal.** People see who reports to whom in their own company: the same tree and chart as the People module, with search. Switch it on, and choose which groups see it, under **System → Self-service** ([#414](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/414), thanks @JB-SRB).
- **Settings on one page.** Every module's settings page has a **Tabs / One page** switch. One page shows every section with an index down the side and a **Search settings** box. Each person chooses their own ([#399](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/399), thanks @pappas81).
- **Morning checks on a schedule:** every day, weekdays, chosen days of the week, or monthly (a date, the first or last weekday, or the last day). A check that is not due is greyed, never hidden ([#383](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/383), thanks @nebev).
- **Missing information on assets.** Each asset type can say what should be filled in - serial number, location, purchase details, any custom field. Assets that still lack it are flagged and listed, never refused ([#404](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/404), thanks @Dr4inder).
- **Approvers can correct a waiting form request's answers.** Every change is recorded with the old and new answer, who changed it and why ([#298](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/298), thanks @Shadowtech87).
- **"Why is this here?" on every bell row.** The **(i)** says who or what made the change (a person, a workflow, email, a scheduled job), how, when, and why you were told, checked against the ticket's history.
- **Attach a file from Files to an email reply**, and **Edit a person without leaving People** ([#397](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/397), thanks @pappas81). Administrators can make a **new company** straight from the person editor ([#398](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/398)).
- **After signing in, you go back to the page you were on** when your session ended ([#407](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/407), built by @cloudcogsio).
- **Start each sign-in in the company you last used** - an opt-in under Preferences ([#409](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/409), built by @cloudcogsio).
- **Table views reopen with the view you last picked** on Changes, Tickets, Assets, Tasks, Domains and Calendar ([#408](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/408), built by @cloudcogsio).
- **Tasks show their ID** on the board and table, the table has a Company column, and hovering a task on the Calendar or Timeline shows a details card ([#405](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/405), built by @cloudcogsio).
- **Move a change to another company through the REST API**, taking its linked tasks with it ([#390](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/390), built by @cloudcogsio).
- **Docker behind a reverse proxy** can record the visitor's real address, with a screen that explains and tests the setup ([#410](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/410), built by @cloudcogsio).
- **Debug tool D021** shows every bell row raised for a ticket next to the ticket's history, to answer "did that person really do it?" ([#365](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/365)).

## Improvements

- **Watchtower on a wall screen:** each person chooses how often it refreshes (1 to 15 minutes), and every number opens exactly the list it counts ([#388](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/discussions/388), thanks @Shadowtech87).
- **Changes:** the list has proper column headings instead of four unlabelled pills. A change's PDF carries your own logo, the risk matrix, approvals and everything else the change shows. Scrolling a change no longer keeps its whole details panel pinned at the top.
- **Text sizes are consistent across every module**, and the Tickets settings headings follow one scale.
- **The reply and forward window fits the screen**, with the drop zone in view.
- **The header icons sit closer together** and the company drop-down matches the buttons beside it.
- **Checklists and Learning settings** use the same tabs as every other module.

## Fixes

- **The bell named the wrong person.** A change made automatically - by a workflow, or mail arriving while someone had their inbox open - was announced as done by that person, and their own team could miss the notification. The bell now names what really made the change, and project reminders say "by FreeITSM" instead of "Someone" ([#365](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/365), thanks @duongtuanan).
- **A change with no type, priority or status stopped the Changes list drawing.**
- **Two Watchtower cards counted things their list could never show** (Contracts in every company, and projects counted twice).
- **The ticket right-click menu's last item** could be below the bottom of the window.
- **The reading pane was a narrow strip on tablets and small laptops** ([#413](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/413), thanks @cloudcogsio).
- **The sign-in card touched both edges of a phone screen** ([#412](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/412), fixed by @cloudcogsio).
- **Some header buttons could not be reached** when a module's buttons shrank to icons ([#411](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/411), fixed by @cloudcogsio).

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Sign in as an administrator and run **System → Database Verification**. It adds the Consumables tables, the asset expected-information tables, and the new columns for schedules and preferences.
3. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5).
4. **Optional, Docker behind a reverse proxy:** to record visitors' real addresses, set `TRUSTED_PROXIES` in `docker-compose.yml` and check it under **System → Docker → Behind a reverse proxy**. Nothing changes until you do.

## Rollback

Safe to go back to 5.7.0: this release only adds tables and columns, which 5.7.0 ignores. Things to know if you do:
- Workflows with a branch step will not run their branches.
- Morning checks with a schedule are due every day again.
- Consumables stays in the database but has no screen until you upgrade again.
