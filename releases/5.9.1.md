## What this release is about

This release fixes two security problems, both reported privately by @M4ru-0x03 on 19 November 2026. **If you use departments to keep some tickets away from part of the service desk, or run FreeITSM for more than one company, upgrade today.** There is nothing to do beyond upgrading. Alongside them, a Knowledge article's PDF now keeps its formatting and pictures, and four smaller fixes ride along.

## Security

| Date | What happened |
|---|---|
| 19 Nov 2026 | Both problems reported privately by @M4ru-0x03, with a proof of concept and a suggested fix |
| 22 Nov 2026 | Fixed and released in 5.9.1 |

- **Analysts could change tickets in departments their team cannot see.** Since 5.1.0, an analyst in a team only *sees* the tickets of that team's departments. Seeing a ticket was checked properly, but changing one was not: an analyst who knew a ticket's number could still reassign it, change its department or sensitivity, add notes and time, split it, delete it and restore it. Because a ticket's owner can always see it, an analyst could make themselves the owner of, say, a confidential HR ticket and then read it in full. This affected the screens and the REST API alike. Changing a ticket now checks the department rule as well, everywhere. **Affected: 5.1.0 to 5.9.0.** Installs where nobody is in a team linked to a department, and administrators, were not affected.
- **On a multi-company install, an analyst could read and change another company's asset custom fields.** The asset page's custom fields did not check the asset's company, so an analyst limited to some companies could read the custom field values of any other company's assets, overwrite them, and add or remove their field sets. Every other part of an asset already checked this. **Affected: every release with custom asset fields, up to 5.9.0.** Single-company installs were not affected.

Thank you to @M4ru-0x03 for clear reports, a working proof of concept and a suggested fix for each.

## Improvements

- **Pictures embedded in an article sent through the REST API now meet the same size limit as uploaded pictures** (**Knowledge → Settings → Images**). Before, a picture placed inside the article's HTML skipped the limit. A picture that is too large gets a clear 413 response naming which picture it is, its size and the limit, and nothing is saved. Pictures already in an article are not checked again, so existing articles stay editable. Pictures added in the article editor are never affected.

## Fixes

- **A Knowledge article's PDF lost its formatting and its pictures.** Line breaks, bold, italic, underline, links, nested list bullets and numbers, quotes and the smaller headings now come through, and pictures - embedded in the article or linked from another site - are drawn. A picture that cannot be read leaves a short note instead of vanishing. **Email (Link + PDF)** attaches the same, better PDF ([#423](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/423), thanks @proseriox).
- **Scrolling a Knowledge article on a computer, the text showed through a strip above the pinned title.** The title now sits flush.
- **The Attach SOP checklists workflow action showed "No matching checklists"**, so no checklist could be picked and nothing was attached ([#427](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/427), thanks @pappas81).
- **Open in inbox on the tickets calendar did not open the ticket** ([#422](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/422), thanks @pappas81).
- **A form question with its label beside the answer left a wide gap after a short label.** The label now takes only the room it needs ([#425](https://github.com/edmozley/freeitsm/issues/425), thanks @pappas81).

## Upgrading

1. Pull the new code, or rebuild the Docker image.
2. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl-F5) - this release updates cached JavaScript and styles. No Database Verification is needed.

Rollback: safe - this release does not change the database.
